# Milo

> You picked the person. Milo handles the plan. Milo plans a night out. Tell Milo the mood, and Milo shapes the plan.

## Status

- Milo is an early prototype and Bengaluru-first.
- Milo is not a dating app.
- Today you can try the prototype in your browser: pick moods, swipe ideas, build a night, seal it with a time, add it to your calendar and share it on WhatsApp.
- Booking and reminders are coming soon.
- Milo does not take payments.

## Quick answers

**What is Milo?**
Milo plans a night out. Tell it your mood, and it shapes a dinner, a walk and a dessert you can send on WhatsApp.

**Is it a dating app?**
No. You already found your person. Milo is just for the plan.

**Where does it work?**
Milo is Bengaluru-first, and early. You can try the prototype in your browser today.

**What can I do today?**
Pick moods, swipe ideas, build a night, seal it with a time, add it to your calendar and share it on WhatsApp.

**Can Milo book or pay for me?**
Not yet. Milo makes the plan and you book it. Booking is coming soon. Milo doesn't take payments.

**What happens to my answers?**
In this prototype, they stay in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server.
