Sound familiar?

Planning a good date is weirdly hard. Not because you don't care. Because you care a lot.

  • the anywhere's-fine

    "so, where do you want to go?" "anywhere's fine!" forty minutes later, still anywhere.

  • the 4.6-star shout

    spent an hour on google maps. picked the place with 4.6 stars. it was loud. we shouted our feelings.

  • the unasked question

    i wanted to ask what she'd like. didn't. what if i got it wrong by asking?

  • the pizza standoff

    she wanted me to plan something nice. i wanted her to just say what she wanted. we ordered pizza.

  • the 10 pm dessert

    booked dinner. forgot nothing about the restaurant. forgot the dessert place closes at 10.

  • the four-tab plan

    the plan lived in four chats, two tabs and one very confident voice note.

You're not bad at dates. You've just been planning alone, with too many tabs.

Asking feels risky. Guessing feels worse. So it's "anywhere's fine" again.
